Privacy policy.

This site is a static personal portfolio. It does not run a backend, does not use a database, and does not set tracking cookies.

What this site collects

Theme preference (light or dark) is stored in your browser's local storage so it persists between visits. That's the only piece of data this site stores about you, and it never leaves your browser.

Contact form

The contact form on this site opens your own email client with a pre-filled message via a mailto: link. No form data is transmitted to or stored on any server operated by this site.

Third-party fonts

This site loads Space Grotesk, Inter, and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts. Loading fonts from Google's servers may expose your IP address to Google, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Analytics

This site does not currently run any analytics or advertising scripts. If that changes, this page will be updated to reflect it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@cloudpath.dev.
